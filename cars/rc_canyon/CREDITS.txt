rc_canyon — RayCity car package

3D model: "2023 GMC Canyon AT4X" by Ddiaz Design
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/2023-gmc-canyon-at4x-372b6c66794649499c7bffbb07888609

Changes (--bake mode): rebuilt as a low-poly outer shell (about 2,900 triangles at LOD 2), split into tuning parts,
details (grille, badges, lights, windows) baked from the original model into the texture, wheels removed (the game adds its own).

Built with car-generator/tools/build-car.mjs (--bake) using escarabajo as the layout template.
